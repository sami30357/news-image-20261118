One 1024×1024 front-page poster summing up Wednesday 18 November 2026 — 48 stories across 7 regions (Asia 7, Europe 6, Americas 11, Africa 7, Middle East 6, Oceania 5, Global 6), drawn in deep teal and amber on cream paper. A masthead names the date, a region bar counts the stories, a "Day in UTC" strip times the day from 01:21 to 22:11, three large drawn panels lead, ten numbered cards with small drawn pictures follow, a dotted world map anchors 35 numbered briefs, a numbers strip and a date-check box close the page. It reads at three sizes: the date and the three leads at thumbnail, the 13 pictures at half size, all 48 stories at full size. `main.ts` copies the poster to the output directory; `index.html` shows it with the story list.
