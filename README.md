# The World in One Picture — 18 November 2026 (Evening Edition, V5)

One front page holding a whole day of news: 52 verified stories from Wednesday 18 November 2026, spread across 7 regions and told through 15 drawn pictures. A charcoal "Evening Edition" masthead opens the page, three lead stories get full pictures at the top, and a dotted world map band pins the 15 drawn stories to where they happened. Every story carries a proof tag, a HOW SURE legend explains what CONFIRMED, CLAIM, OFFICIALS and FORECAST mean, a UTC strip walks the day hour by hour, and a DATE CHECK box keeps out anything first reported on another day.

Files: `entry.png`, `main.ts`, `index.html`, `package.json`.

`main.ts` copies `entry.png` to `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`.
